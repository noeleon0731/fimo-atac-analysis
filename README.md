## Independent Project: Examining the association between chromatin accessibility and variants implicated in phenotypic variation in Drosophila melanogaster.
*Noelle Ramos, Dr. Lorissa Saiz, Dr. Erin Kelleher*

    Software: Rstudio (GenomicRanges Library), MEME Suite FIMO (JASPAR TF database), Oo-site (Rangan Lab)

### Contents
   (1) Used MEME Suite FIMO to scan for binding sites corresponding with regions of differential chromatin accessibility in our ROI.

   (2) Analyzed single-cell RNAseq expression data for developmental expression patterns of candidate transcription factors.

   (3) Compared predicted binding site motifs among sterile and fertile founder alleles
